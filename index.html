01 / LEARNING ROADMAP

Business Analyst / Core Path

Learn to move from an unclear business problem to a solution people can use—and prove it made a difference.

STARTING POINTBeginner friendly
CORE MISSIONS8 connected stages
CAPSTONEPortfolio-ready case
YOUR SIGNAL PROGRESS0 / 8 stages complete

Progress is saved in this browser. Open any stage to view its topics and practice output.

  1. 01FOUNDATION / FRAME THE CONTEXTBusiness context & analyst mindsetUnderstand what the organization is trying to achieve before proposing a fix.STAGE 01

    Topics to map

    Business models & valueStrategy and objectivesKPIs and outcomesProblem vs. symptomEthics and assumptions
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A one-page problem brief with the goal, affected users, current evidence, and success measure.

  2. 02DISCOVERY / FIND THE RIGHT PROBLEMStakeholders & discoveryFind the people, constraints, and evidence that shape the opportunity.STAGE 02

    Topics to map

    Stakeholder mappingInterviews & workshopsObservation & surveysEmpathy and user needsScope and context diagrams
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A stakeholder map and discovery plan that separates known facts from open questions.

  3. 03DEFINITION / MAKE NEEDS TESTABLEElicitation & requirementsTurn what people need into clear, testable statements.STAGE 03

    Topics to map

    Functional & non-functional needsUser stories and use casesAcceptance criteriaPrioritization (MoSCoW)Traceability and change control
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A prioritized requirement set with acceptance criteria and links back to stakeholder needs.

  4. 04ANALYSIS / SEE HOW WORK REALLY FLOWSProcess & root-cause analysisModel the current experience, find friction, and design a better future state.STAGE 04

    Topics to map

    As-is / to-be workflowsSwimlanes & BPMN basicsRoot-cause analysisWaste, handoffs & bottlenecksRisks and dependencies
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A current-state and future-state process map with the root cause and expected improvement called out.

  5. 05EVIDENCE / LET THE DATA TEST THE STORYData & decision analysisUse fit-for-purpose evidence to size the problem and compare outcomes.STAGE 05

    Topics to map

    Measures and data qualityExcel / spreadsheet analysisSQL fundamentalsDescriptive statisticsCharts and insight narratives
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A small evidence pack: baseline, key finding, caveats, and a clear view of what the data cannot tell you.

  6. 06EVALUATION / RECOMMEND A VIABLE CHANGESolution options & business caseCompare feasible choices by value, cost, risk, and impact.STAGE 06

    Topics to map

    Option analysisFeasibility and constraintsCosts, benefits & assumptionsRisk and impact assessmentRecommendation framing
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A concise business case comparing options and recommending one measurable, low-risk next step.

  7. 07DELIVERY / HELP THE CHANGE LANDAgile delivery & verificationWork with delivery teams to build the right thing and confirm it works.STAGE 07

    Topics to map

    Backlogs & refinementAgile / iterative deliveryUAT plans and test scenariosDefect triageChange readiness & adoption
    FIELD OUTPUT

    A delivery-ready slice with acceptance checks, UAT evidence, and a plan to support affected users.

  8. 08OUTCOMES / PROVE THE VALUEBenefits, communication & capstoneMeasure what changed, explain why it matters, and package the work.FINAL STAGE

    Topics to map

    Benefits realizationOutcome measures & dashboardsExecutive storytellingLessons learnedPortfolio case study
    CAPSTONE SIGNAL

    Present a complete case from problem brief to measured recommendation, including artifacts and trade-offs.
